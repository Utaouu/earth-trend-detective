Become the detective

Investigation questions

1. Observations: What pattern do you notice in the observed LST time series? Are there missing periods?
2. Trend: How does the fitted baseline differ from individual observations? What does the confidence interval tell you?
3. Anomaly: Why might an observation differ substantially from the fitted pattern? What additional evidence would you need?
4. Data quality: Could spatial coverage or incomplete QA information affect your interpretation?
5. Comparison: What similarities and differences do you notice across the urban, forest and desert environments?
